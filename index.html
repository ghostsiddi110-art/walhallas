Our Story

Walhallas Marshal FC was born in 2024 on the streets of Pakistan. A group of young footballers, led by our founder Ghost, decided to stop dreaming and start building — with nothing but raw talent and unshakable brotherhood.

Our mission: "Rising from the streets to conquer." We aim to become Pakistan's most recognised football brand — a symbol of courage, unity, and ambition.

We are more than a team. We are a movement. Welcome to the Marshal family.
